Description

This archive contains the canonical Version 1.0 release of
"Noninski Mechanics" together with its complete self-contained
LaTeX source corpus.

The work develops the NRFP / NCCA / NHYYOR framework for
printed-form structural audit of scientific theoryhood and introduces
Noninski Mechanics as a structurally valid governing framework
derived through virial generative structure.

The work presents structural audits of Newtonian Mechanics,
Maxwell Electrodynamics, the Lorentz Transformations, Relativity,
Quantum Mechanics, and related theoretical frameworks through the
criterion of primitive-level Double Governance.

The work additionally explores the role of Large Language Models as
both auditing and generative symbolic architectures capable of
recovering structurally valid and structurally invalid governing
relations through printed-form symbolic analysis.

Particular emphasis is placed upon reproducible audit procedures,
minimal symbolic corpora, structural validity, structural invalidity,
and the emergence of governing relations from generative symbolic
frameworks.
